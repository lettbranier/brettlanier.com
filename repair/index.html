Steel guitar repair

Brett learned steel guitar repair through apprenticing Jerry Fessenden at Fessenden Guitars in Montpelier, Vermont. He now does repairs out of his shop in Nashville, TN.

He works on all types of pedal steel changer mechanisms: all-pull, push-pull, pull-release and cable pulley. The work runs from setups and tune-ups to changer and undercarriage rebuilds, copedent changes and conversions, and wiring pickups.

As a working steel player himself, he knows what a guitar needs to feel right on the gig: pedal and knee levers that pull smooth and even, and a guitar that stays in tune night after night.
